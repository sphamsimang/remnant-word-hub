# Msimang Bible Reading

A clean, distraction-free 3-Column Study Bible web application featuring **KJV**, **NKJV**, and **Amplified Bible, Classic Edition (AMPC)** in single or parallel reading, **Study Bible Historical & Chapter Context**, **Tyndale Open Study Notes**, **100% Verbatim Original Strong's + Brown-Driver-Briggs (1906) / Thayer's (1889) Lexicon**, a **365-Day Reading Plan & Finish Date Tracker**, and **Personal Reflections** synced across phone and computer via Supabase.

## Features

1. **Left Column — 365-Day Reading Plan & Completion Date Tracker**
   - Tracks all **1,189 chapters** across 365 daily tiles starting from your chosen start date.
   - Live **Projected Finish Date** indicator updates dynamically as you mark chapters and daily tiles complete.

2. **Center Column — Study Bible Context & Parallel KJV / NKJV / Classic AMPC**
   - **Study Bible Introduction & Chapter Context** right above every chapter: **Who Wrote It**, **Written To (Audience)**, **Date & Place**, **Historical & Cultural Context of the Times**, **Chapter Context**, and interactive **Chapter Geography Map**.
   - **Inline Study Bible Notes** (*Tyndale Open Study Notes* / *Jamieson-Fausset-Brown*) toggled with one click.
   - Read **NKJV**, **KJV (with Strong's)**, or **AMPC (Amplified Bible, Classic Edition)** alone or side-by-side (`NKJV + AMPC`, `KJV + NKJV`, `KJV + AMPC`, or all three `KJV + NKJV + AMPC`).

3. **Right Column — Simple Personal Notes & Verbatim Original Lexicon**
   - **Minimal Notes Pad:** Click any verse to jot down reflections and optional `#Topic` tags (building your own personal topic tags purely from what you study), synced to Supabase Cloud.
   - **100% Original Scholarly Lexicon:** Displays verbatim **Brown-Driver-Briggs Hebrew & English Lexicon (1906)**, **Thayer's Greek-English Lexicon (1889)**, and **James Strong's Exhaustive Concordance (1890)** — the exact original sources behind Blue Letter Bible.
